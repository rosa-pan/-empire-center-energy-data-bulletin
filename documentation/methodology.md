# Methodology

## Residential Prices

Both electricity and natural gas workflows use residential prices only.

## Geographic Coverage

Included:
- 50 states
- United States

Excluded:
- District of Columbia
- Census regions
- Census divisions

## Missing Data

When the latest month is unavailable for a state,
the workflow uses the most recent available prior observation.

## Comparison States

The following states are used in Prices & Dynamics tables:

- California
- Connecticut
- Florida
- Massachusetts
- New Jersey
- New York
- Pennsylvania
- Texas
- Vermont
- United States

## 2019 Baseline

Current prices are compared to the same calendar month in 2019.

## Rolling Averages

Rolling averages use the most recent 12 months,
including the current month.
